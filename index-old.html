🖼️ PPDT Picture Practice

These are original practice illustrations made for this app. Try observing each picture briefly, note the people, setting and situation, then create your own realistic story.

Picture 01

Notice Board

Observe the setting and build your own story from what you perceive.

Picture 02

Group Planning

Focus on characters, relationships and the action taking place.

Picture 03

Bridge Scene

Look for a practical situation, then form a logical beginning, action and outcome.

Picture 04

Planning Board

Use the picture only as a prompt; your story should be your own interpretation.

⏱️ Practice idea: spend a short observation period on one picture, write your story, and then practise a concise narration before discussing it with a study group.

🎖️ Real-Time SSB Simulation

A timed practice simulation based on the official SSB structure. It is a training tool, not an official SSB test or a prediction of selection.

Compressed training mode: the real SSB spans multiple days; this simulator runs the phases continuously so you can practise the sequence in one sitting.
